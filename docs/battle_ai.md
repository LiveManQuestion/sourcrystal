# Battle AI layout

How the trainer AI is organized (see also `CLAUDE.md`).

- `engine/battle/ai/move.asm`: `AIChooseMove` runs the scoring layers enabled
  in each trainer class's `TRNATTR_AI_MOVE_WEIGHTS`
  (`data/trainers/attributes.asm`). Lower score = better; `AIDiscourageMove`
  adds 10. Layer order: `AIScoringPointers`. `AI_Smogon` runs last.
- `engine/battle/ai/scoring.asm`: the vanilla-style layers (AI_Basic, AI_Smart,
  AI_Status, ...). AI_Smart's per-effect handlers still apply under AI_Smogon,
  which adds to or cancels their scores. AI_Status covers status immunities.
- `engine/battle/ai/smogon.asm` (`AI_Smogon`): the GSC OU strategy layer,
  only active in Challenge Mode (`wChallengeMode` bit `GAME_CHALLENGE_MODE_F`).
  - Per-effect handlers are in `AI_Smogon_EffectHandlers`. A handler gets
    `hl` = the move's score and may clobber everything else.
    `AISmogon_Encourage` / `AISmogon_GreatlyEncourage` / `AISmogon_EncourageNear`
    lower the score.
  - It only uses what a human player could know: the player's active mon,
    the moves it has revealed (`wPlayerUsedMoves`), and per-species OU
    tendencies (`SmogonTraits` in `data/battle/ai/smogon_traits.asm`, flags
    `SMOGON_*` in `constants/trainer_data_constants.asm`) and usual attacks
    (`SmogonExpectedAttacks`, used until four moves are revealed). Both are
    generated by `tools/claude/smogon_data.py` (`SMOGON_THREAT` is kept).
  - After the handlers, `AI_Smogon_Asleep`: fast asleep (sleep counter 2+)
    with a usable Sleep Talk or Snore, every other move gets
    `AIDiscourageMove`; awake, Sleep Talk and Snore do (AI_Smart encourages
    Sleep Talk unless the counter is 1). Then `AI_Smogon_SureKO` ranks the attacks whose lowest
    roll (counted as 27/32 of `AIDamageCalc`'s highest) KOs the player:
    the most accurate gets `AISmogon_GreatlyEncourage`, the other KOs +2.
    A priority attack ranks first when the player is faster; Lock-On and
    Thunder in rain count as sure hits; charging, recharging, locking and
    random-damage attacks and Explosion are left out (`.NotSureKOEffects`),
    and nothing counts through a Substitute or while the enemy is asleep
    or frozen (Sleep Talk keeps its turn). The ranking is
    `AISmogon_RankLoadedMove`: it clears `wCriticalHit` while it
    calculates (the flag stays set after a critical hit, and `AIDamageCalc`,
    also used by the vanilla AI_Aggressive and AI_Risky, would double the
    damage) and keeps `wCurDamage`, which Counter reads. Costs about 2
    frames a turn.
- `engine/battle/ai/smogon_switch.asm`: matchup scoring as a KO race.
  `AISmogon_AttackDamage` estimates each side's best attack with the damage
  formula (no random factor, crits or accuracy), `AISmogon_TurnsToKO` turns
  it into hits needed from current HP, and the score is
  `SMOGON_MATCHUP_EVEN` + 16 × log2(the player's half turns / ours): the
  faster side gets half a turn, and a switch-in takes a hit on the way in
  (not when replacing a fainted Pokemon) and, with Spikes on the AI's side,
  loses 1/8 of its max HP unless it's a Flying type (also after a faint).
  A Pokemon that can boost safely (`AISmogon_GetSetupBoost`: Swords Dance,
  or Curse, Sharpen, Meditate or Growth; the player can't phaze or Haze;
  `SMOGON_SETUP_SAFE_HITS` of the player's strongest attack don't KO it,
  or `SMOGON_SD_SAFE_HITS` with Swords Dance)
  counts its attacks of the boosted category at x2 (Swords Dance) or x1.5
  power, a turn later. `AISmogon_ApplyHealing` adds
  healing: Leftovers comes off the damage taken (the player's Pokemon is
  assumed to hold it), and a recovery move (half max HP per turn, Rest a
  quarter) that outheals the damage taken lasts until its recovery PP runs
  out (`AISmogon_SideTurns`: its HP plus each heal left), while its own KO
  takes longer by the turns spent healing, at most its PP
  (`AISmogon_HealDelay`). The AI knows its own Pokemon's PP; the player's
  Pokemon counts its revealed recovery moves, or Rest if its `SmogonTraits`
  say so, with their base PP. Attack PP counts too: each side keeps a list
  of its attacks' damage and PP (`ATTACKS_*`, `AISmogon_AddAttack`), and
  `AISmogon_TurnsWithPP` uses the strongest until its PP runs out, then the
  next; a side that can't get through with all its PP can't KO. The AI's own
  moves use their current PP; the player's moves (revealed attacks, recovery
  moves, and its expected attacks at `SMOGON_UNKNOWN_BASE_PP`) get the PP Ups
  per move across the player's whole party (`AISmogon_CountPlayerPPUps`):
  the AI can tell how much the player uses PP Ups, not on which Pokemon.
  For speed,
  `AISmogon_SetupDamage` precomputes the formula without the power,
  `AISmogon_FillTypeMatchups` fills each attack type's effectiveness from a
  copy of the type chart in the AI bank (`data/types/type_matchups_table.asm`
  is included both there and as `TypeMatchups`), and `AISmogon_Divide24`
  replaces the slow home `Divide`: a switch decision takes about the same
  few frames as before the race model. Also
  `AISmogon_FindBestSwitchIn` and the post-faint replacement choice. Tuning
  constants (`SMOGON_*`) are in `constants/trainer_data_constants.asm`.
- `engine/battle/ai/items.asm`: `AI_SwitchOrTryItem`. `SwitchSmogon` is the
  mid-battle switch decision for `SWITCH_SMOGON` trainer classes. It runs
  after the player's switch, with the move already picked (`wCurEnemyMove`,
  loaded in `wEnemyMoveStruct`), and stays in when that move KOs the player
  first (`AISmogon_PickedMoveKOsFirst`: a sure KO while faster, or with
  priority), unless Perish Song is at 1. It also stays in to land a sleep
  move, Spikes, or Rapid Spin (Spikes on its side and a phazing player)
  first (`AISmogon_PickedLeadMoveFirst`), and below half HP to use a
  Recover, Softboiled or Milk Drink that outheals the player's strongest
  attack (`AISmogon_PickedHealOutpaces`). The active Pokemon's score takes
  the same sleep or freeze penalty as a switch-in
  (`AISmogon_PenalizeAsleepActive`), unless it wakes up this turn: waking
  up costs the turn (`CantMove` in `CheckEnemyTurn`), so a sleep counter of
  N loses N turns, and Rest sets 3. When neither side can KO the other
  (`wSmogonAIActiveStalled`, set by `AISmogon_ScoreActive` when both
  `AISmogon_SideTurns` reach `SMOGON_MAX_TURNS`), any better matchup is
  reason to switch (like `wEnemyAIMovesSpent`), and being able to Haze or
  phaze a boosted player is no reason to stay: Crobat used to Haze a Curse
  Steelix forever. It also
  switches out a Pokemon with no useful move left: `AIChooseMove` (which runs
  first each turn) sets `wEnemyAIMovesSpent` when every move scores above 20.
- Banks: `items.asm`, `switch.asm`, `scoring.asm`, `smogon.asm`,
  `smogon_switch.asm` and the Smogon data tables are all in the "Enemy Trainers"
  section (bank $0e), so they call each other with plain `call`. Battle Core
  (bank $0f) and Effect Commands (bank $0d) are reached with `farcall`/`callfar`
  or `newfarcall` (keeps all registers). `FindAliveEnemyMons` stays in Effect
  Commands (Roar uses it).
- Strategy sources: `smogonairesources/` (untracked): Smogon GSC articles,
  sample teams, the replay stats spreadsheet, GSC dex analyses and move pages (JSON), and
  "GSC - A Naughty Guide" (text).
